# CredEcho — 5-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

**0:00 — Problem (30s).** "Banks can't lend to New-to-Credit MSMEs — no ITR, no
balance sheet, no bureau score. But every MSME leaves a digital trail: GST, UPI,
Account Aggregator, EPFO. CredEcho turns that trail into a lendable score."

**0:30 — Solution (30s).** "We built an explainable ML Financial Health Card:
a 0-100 score, a lending decision, a suggested loan amount and rate — ULI, OCEN
and AA compatible, in under 150 milliseconds."

**1:00 — Dashboard (60s).** Open the Bank Officer dashboard. "Three live
applicants, each already scored. Sri Traders scores 92 — Excellent. QuickMart
scores low — flagged Risky. The officer sees the whole queue ranked."

**2:00 — Health Card (60s).** Click Sri Traders. "Score ring, default
probability, class, suggested ₹23L loan at 11.5%. On the right, the AI
recommendation — Approve, 96% confidence — with a plain-English explanation."

**3:00 — Explainability (45s).** "Every decision is transparent. This SHAP-style
chart shows GST filing consistency and cash-flow stability pushing the score up,
debt ratio pulling it down. RBI-audit friendly."

**3:45 — Radar + What-if (45s).** "Ten dimension scores on the radar. And the
what-if simulator: improve GST filing to 0.99 and watch the score and eligibility
move in real time."

**4:30 — Tech + Impact (30s).** "XGBoost, 98% accuracy on 50k MSMEs, FastAPI,
Next.js, fully Dockerized, mock connectors ready to swap for production ULI/OCEN/AA.
CredEcho expands formal credit access while keeping risk explainable. Thank you."
